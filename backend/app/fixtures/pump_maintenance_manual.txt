================================================================================
SECTION 1 — OVERVIEW
================================================================================

This manual covers canonical asset EQ-001 (display tag P-101), the centrifugal pump assembly installed in Facility A,
cooling-water circuit CW-01. P-101 is a horizontally-split-case centrifugal pump
rated at 250 m³/h at 45 m head, driven by a 55 kW induction motor.

Supplier: AlphaSeals Ltd (supplier code: SUP-001)
Component: Mechanical seal assembly (part SKU: MCS-4420)
Criticality: HIGH — single point of failure on CW-01

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE INTERVALS
================================================================================

2.1 Daily Checks (Operator)
   - Verify suction and discharge pressure gauges are within range (1.5–4.5 bar).
   - Check bearing housing temperature; must not exceed 70 °C.
   - Inspect mechanical seal for drips; zero leakage is acceptable; >3 drops/min
     requires immediate work order.
   - Record vibration reading from sensor VS-P101; alarm threshold is 7.5 mm/s RMS.

2.2 Quarterly Maintenance (Technician — every 90 days)
   - Lubricate bearing housing with Mobil SHC 639 grease, 20 g per bearing.
   - Pump P-101 requires bearing lubrication every 90 days using the
     manufacturer-specified lubricant grade NLGI-2.
   - Inspect coupling alignment; maximum allowable angular misalignment: 0.05 mm/m.
   - Replace mechanical seal if wear exceeds 1.5 mm measured at the carbon face.

2.3 Annual Overhaul (Maintenance team)
   - Full disassembly and inspection of impeller, wear rings, and shaft sleeve.
   - Hydrostatic test at 1.5× rated discharge pressure.
   - Re-align motor and pump shafts to within 0.02 mm TIR.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — COMMON FAILURE MODES
================================================================================

3.1 Mechanical Seal Failure
   Failure mode: Loss of containment / shaft leakage
   Root cause:   Dry running (suction loss), thermal shock, misalignment
   Component:    Mechanical seal assembly (MCS-4420), supplied by AlphaSeals Ltd
   Supplier code: SUP-001
   MTBF under normal operation: 18 months
   Corrective action: Isolate pump, replace seal kit per IB-WI-MCS-4420.

3.2 Bearing Failure
   Failure mode: High vibration, elevated temperature, noise
   Root cause:   Inadequate lubrication, contaminated grease, overloading
   MTBF: 36 months when lubricated on schedule
   Corrective action: Replace bearings; check alignment and balance.

3.3 Impeller Erosion
   Failure mode: Reduced flow, cavitation noise
   Root cause:   Particulates in fluid, low NPSH available
   Corrective action: Replace impeller; install upstream strainer with 500 µm mesh.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — SAFETY
================================================================================

- De-energize and lock-out/tag-out motor before any mechanical work (IB-HS-001).
- Drain and flush pump before opening casing; CW-01 may contain biocide.
- Use PPE: safety glasses, nitrile gloves, steel-toe boots.
